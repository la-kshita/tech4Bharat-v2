## PCB inspection literature

- Domingues, T., et al. (2023), [Automatic printed circuit board inspection: a comprehensible survey](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44163-023-00081-5), *Discover Artificial Intelligence*.
- Ling, Q. and Isa, N. A. M. (2023), [Printed Circuit Board Defect Detection Methods Based on Image Processing, Machine Learning and Deep Learning: A Survey](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3246008), *IEEE Access* 11, 15921–15944.
- Zou, Y., et al. (2022), [SPot-the-Difference Self-Supervised Pre-training for Anomaly Detection and Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.14315), ECCV 2022; source repository [amazon-science/spot-diff](https://github.com/amazon-science/spot-diff).
- Tang, S., He, F., Huang, X., and Yang, J. (2019), [Online PCB Defect Detector On A New PCB Defect Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.06197); source repository [tangsanli5201/DeepPCB](https://github.com/tangsanli5201/DeepPCB).
- Villa Brochero, B., Gibson, I., and Talavera, E. (2026), [PCB-MC: Missing Component Analysis in Printed Circuit Boards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39427), arXiv preprint.
- [PCB-SAID: A Low-Cost Camera-Based Dataset for Few-Shot SMD Assembly Inspection](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCVW69036.2025.00145), ICCV Workshops 2025.
