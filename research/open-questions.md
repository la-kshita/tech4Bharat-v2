# Research questions still open

## Enterprise and buyer discovery

- Which PCB defects lead to buyer rejection, rework, or warranty costs?
- What rejection baseline and time period can a unit share?
- What evidence does the buyer accept as proof of inspection, and who approves it?
- Who performs visual checks today, and how much inspection time is available per board?
- Who can provide qualified defect labels and approved board/revision references?

## Inspection conditions and scope

- Which single board model/revision and side/view should the pilot cover?
- What lighting, space, phone camera, distance, and mounting are available at the line?
- Are guided close-ups needed for small components, polarity marks, or placement checks?
- How often do board revisions or component substitutions occur?
- Which cases must return “cannot inspect” or require a manual check?

## Evaluation and adoption

- What missed-issue rate, false-flag rate, coverage, capture time, and review time would be acceptable to the unit and buyer?
- Does the batch record contain enough evidence to support a buyer's decision?
- Who pays: the PCB unit, a sourcing buyer, or another partner? What commercial model would fit the actual workflow?
- What device, connectivity, language, training, retention, and privacy constraints apply on site?

No enterprise interviews or buyer acceptance results are claimed in this repository. Keep these as questions until evidence is collected.
